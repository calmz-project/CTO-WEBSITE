01 — The Story

A launch with no pitch deck. Just a dare.

Nobody was put in charge.

$CTO went out with one instruction — take it over — and nothing else decided in advance. The group picked the name and the picture. It set the supply at one billion and the tax at three and three. It drew the max-wallet line itself.

Then the entire allocation went into the liquidity pool and the keys went to no one. There is no treasury to spend, no team allocation to unlock, no quarter on a roadmap to miss.

It has been up and it has been down, and it has been discussed at length by people who have never held a single token. Through all of it the group has done the one thing a group can do that a founder cannot: stay.
