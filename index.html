01 · The Story

A launch with no pitch deck. Just a dare.

Nobody was put in charge.

$CTO went out with one instruction, take it over, and nothing else decided in advance. The group picked the name and the picture. It set the supply at one billion and the tax at three and three. It drew the max-wallet line itself.

Then the entire allocation went into the liquidity pool, and that pool was locked for a year on PinkSale. There is no treasury to spend, no team allocation to unlock, no quarter on a roadmap to miss.

It has been up and it has been down, and it has been discussed at length by people who have never held a single token. Through all of it the group has done the one thing a group can do that a founder cannot: stay.

02 · How It Works

Four plain facts. Verify every one of them yourself.

LiquidityLocked, not held

100% of supply seeded the pool at launch and the LP is locked for a year on PinkSale. No treasury wallet, no vesting cliff.

GovernanceDecided in the open

Name, art, supply, tax, wallet cap: each one was a group call, on the record, before day one.

ScopeNothing pending

No "utility coming soon", no unreleased app. What is here is the whole of it.

ProofPublic ledger

It's a token on BNB Chain. Read the contract, watch the pool, check the holders. Trust nothing on faith.
